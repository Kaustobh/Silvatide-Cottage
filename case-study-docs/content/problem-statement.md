## The Problem & Constraints

Luxury hospitality websites frequently suffer from web-bloat. To convey "luxury," designers rely on massive high-resolution imagery, complex grid layouts, and heavy scroll animations. This often results in slow load times, poor SEO, and high bounce rates—directly hurting booking conversions.

**The core challenge:** How do I translate a bespoke, image-heavy Figma design into a highly performant web experience without losing 1px of its aesthetic feel?

**Constraints:**
- **Strict Visual Fidelity:** The design utilized a rigid 8px spatial grid and a strict 400px maximum width for text readability. These constraints had to be enforced programmatically.
- **Complex Layering:** The Hero section required the word "SILVA" to sit *behind* the cabin roof but *in front* of the background trees—a masking effect that is notoriously difficult to achieve responsively without resorting to flattened, inaccessible images.
