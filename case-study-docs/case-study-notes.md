# Case Study Notes

**What should go inside:** Raw braindumps, interview notes (from answering my questions), and high-level thoughts about the project's evolution.
**Why it matters:** It serves as a scratchpad so you don't lose key insights before formalizing them into the final case study.
**Evidence to gather:** Any rough notes on the timeline, major blockers, and "aha" moments during development.
**How it supports the final case study:** Provides the raw narrative fuel for your storytelling.
